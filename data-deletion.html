Delete your account and data

Last updated: 13 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your Tikta account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This applies to customer, merchant and driver accounts.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@tikta.dz with the subject Delete my Tikta account.
  2. Include the phone number you use to sign in to Tikta.
  3. We will send a verification code to that phone number to confirm the request really comes from you.
  4. Once you confirm, we delete your account and data within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

Deleting your account is permanent. You will lose your order history, saved addresses and any unused credit, and you will need to create a new account to use Tikta again.

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, phone number, email address and password.
  • Your saved delivery addresses and location history.
  • Your device and push notification tokens.
  • Your notification history and messaging preferences.
  • For drivers: uploaded documents, vehicle details and location history.

What we keep, and why

  • Order and payment records are kept for the period required by accounting and tax law. We remove your name and contact details from them wherever the law allows.
  • Records needed to prevent fraud or handle a dispute are kept until the matter is closed.

WhatsApp messages

Messages we sent to your phone number through WhatsApp stay in your own WhatsApp chat until you delete them there. After your account is deleted we stop sending you messages.

Questions

If you need help with a deletion request, write to support@tikta.dz. For more on how we handle your information, see our Privacy Policy.
