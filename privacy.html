Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Tikta ("Tikta", "we", "us") collects when you use the Tikta customer, merchant and driver apps and related websites (together, the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, phone number, password, preferred language and, if you add one, your email address.
  • Delivery details: delivery addresses, map locations you select, and notes you leave for the store or the driver.
  • Orders: the items you order, the stores you order from, prices, and your order history.
  • Payment details: the payment method you choose for each order. If you pay by card, the card is processed by our payment provider; we do not store your full card number or security code.
  • Merchant details: store name, address, opening hours, catalogue, and the contact details of the people who manage the store.
  • Driver details: identity and vehicle documents, vehicle information, work schedule and earnings records.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you contact support.

Information collected when you use the Service

  • Location: customers can share their location to find nearby stores and set a delivery address. Drivers share their precise location while they are on shift so that orders can be assigned and tracked.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, app version, language, and a push notification token.
  • Usage and diagnostics: log data such as the time of requests, errors and your IP address, used to keep the Service secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in, including sending a one-time verification code to your phone number.
  • To take, prepare, dispatch and deliver orders, and to show you live order tracking.
  • To send you service messages about your account and your orders by push notification, WhatsApp, SMS or email.
  • To process payments, refunds, merchant settlements and driver earnings.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the Service secure.
  • To understand how the Service is used and improve it.
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

3. WhatsApp messages

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform, provided by Meta, to send verification codes and order updates to the phone number on your account. To do this we share your phone number and the content of the message with Meta. We only send service messages related to your account and your orders. Meta handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account information for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days, except for records we must keep for longer, such as order and payment records required for accounting, tax and fraud prevention.

6. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been accessed without your permission.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and edit most of your information in the app, or ask us for a copy.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete your account and data. See Data deletion.
  • Location: you can turn location access on or off in your device settings. Drivers need it on to receive deliveries.
  • Notifications: you can turn push notifications off in your device settings. We may still send messages that are needed to deliver the Service, such as verification codes.
  • Objection: you can object to a particular use of your information by contacting us.

We handle personal information in line with Algerian Law No. 18-07 on the protection of individuals in the processing of personal data.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

10. Contact us

For any question or request about your personal information, write to support@tikta.dz.
